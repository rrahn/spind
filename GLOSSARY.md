# School locker booking

The Förderverein operates a school locker booking service for parents and their
children. Bookings apply to a specific school year.

## People

**Operator**:
The Förderverein that operates the booking service and contracts with parents.
_Avoid_: School (when referring to the contracting organisation)

**Guardian**:
A parent or other authorised person who manages a child's locker booking and
provides a verified contact email address.
_Avoid_: Registrant, client

**Pupil**:
A child for whom a guardian manages a locker booking.
_Avoid_: Account (a pupil is not the guardian's login)

## Inventory and booking

**Locker unit**:
A physical cabinet containing individually bookable locker compartments.
_Avoid_: Locker (when referring to the entire cabinet)

**Locker**:
One individually bookable compartment within a locker unit.
_Avoid_: Unit, box

**Hold**:
A temporary, exclusive reservation of a locker during the booking process,
lasting 30 minutes from selection without automatic extension.
_Avoid_: Booking (a hold does not grant paid use)

**Booking**:
A pupil's entitlement to use a particular locker during a specific school year.
Renewal creates a separate booking for the next school year.
_Avoid_: Payment, hold

**School year**:
The named annual period to which a pupil's class placement and locker booking
apply.
_Avoid_: Rolling rental period

**Class**:
A named pupil group for a particular school year, such as 7A, 7B, 7N, or Q1,
whose classroom is managed by the operator.
_Avoid_: Grade (when the class subdivision affects the room)

**Classroom**:
The room associated with a class for the relevant school year and used to guide
nearby locker selection.
_Avoid_: Locker location

**Placement**:
A pupil's membership of a named class for one school year.
_Avoid_: Grade (a placement includes the particular class and year)

**Locker slot**:
A particular locker's allocation for one school year, independent of temporary
holds and paid bookings.
_Avoid_: Compartment (a slot is annual; a compartment is physical)

**Booking window**:
The period during which eligible guardians may make bookings for a school year.
_Avoid_: School year (access dates and booking dates are different)
